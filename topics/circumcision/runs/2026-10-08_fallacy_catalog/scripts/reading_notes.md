# Reading notes (rules the reviewer applied)

These are the working rules used on top of the catalog's METHOD.md, kept here so the read can be repeated.

- Read every chunk (`make_chunks.py`, 174 chunks) in order. Record flags with `add_flag.py`, which rejects any quote that is not an exact substring of the snapshot.
- Flag only reasoning in the article's own voice. When the article reports, quotes or attributes an argument to someone else, that is not a flag.
- Sections titled as one side's case (for example "Arguments Supporting Circumcision", "Arguments Against", "Challenges to the Universal Harm Assumption") count as reported material. They are flagged only where the article clearly argues in its own voice beyond that side's case.
- Loaded or charged wording that no inference rests on is not a flag.
- Wrong figures, dates and other factual slips are not fallacies. They were noted separately and are not counted.
- An observational-to-causal health claim is not flagged when the mechanism is well known (for example UTI or penile cancer and the foreskin).
- Using adult African HIV trial results as if they settle infant circumcision, or low-prevalence settings, is flagged as secundum-quid (applying a rule beyond its scope).
- Where the article contradicts itself and the contradiction carries an argument, it is flagged as inconsistency. A plain number or date mismatch counts as a factual slip.
- Entries with text_detectable "no" are never used. For "partly" entries, the outside-text condition must be met from the article itself or common knowledge, otherwise the verdict is "possible issue".
- Side ("favors"): male circumcision articles: pro = favours the practice, anti = against it. FGM articles: anti = against the practice; pro = defends it or plays down harm (including the benign male/female distinction). neutral = helps neither side clearly.
- No earlier run (runs/2026-10-01_*, 2026-10-02_*) and no substance lens was looked at before or during the read.
