## What the numbers mean

- **971 pro arguments** in the 39 male circumcision articles (sentences an AI model labeled pro in step 5; they come from 32 of the 39 articles).
- **281 (29%)** rest on the HIV/STI claim or the cancer claim: 254 on HIV/STI, 27 on cancer only.
- **88 (9%)** had already been flagged by the audit (lost at least one point at step 5, pass 2 or pass 3).
- **602 (62%)** are left: other medical claims 386, religion or culture 105, ethics or law 72, other 39.
- **What-if, not a finding.** The count takes the posts' points as premises; it does not test them. Premise 1 (no HIV/STI protection) contradicts the randomized trials the articles cite (Auvert 2005, Bailey 2007, Gray 2007).
- **AI-tagged, not blind.** The tags were made by AI model sessions, not a person, from a conversation that already knew the earlier results.

## Limits: a narrow selection

The percentage comes from a narrow selection of arguments: the claims in two X posts, plus one point about cancer, applied to the pro arguments in 39 male circumcision Grokipedia articles. It is a what-if count. Applying more premises or arguments would change the numbers, in either direction. Nothing here has been tested beyond that selection.
